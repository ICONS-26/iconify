Practice Questions

Attempt each question, then tap "Show Answer" to check your work and read the explanation.

Q1. A shopkeeper bought a table for ₹1,200 and sold it at a 15% profit. What was the selling price?

Easy
Answer: ₹1,380. Selling Price = Cost Price × (1 + Profit%) = 1200 × 1.15 = ₹1,380.

Q2. A can finish a task in 12 days and B can finish the same task in 18 days. Working together, how many days will they take?

Medium
Answer: 7.2 days. Combined rate = 1/12 + 1/18 = 5/36 of the task per day, so time taken = 36/5 = 7.2 days.

Q3. Find the next number in the series: 3, 8, 15, 24, 35, ?

Medium
Answer: 48. The differences between terms are 5, 7, 9, 11 (consecutive odd numbers). The next difference is 13, so 35 + 13 = 48.

Q4. If CODING is coded as DPEJOH, how would FLOWER be coded using the same rule?

Hard
Answer: GMPXFS. Each letter is shifted one place forward in the alphabet (C→D, O→P, D→E...). Applying the same shift to F-L-O-W-E-R gives G-M-P-X-F-S.

Q5. Choose the word most nearly opposite in meaning to "Frugal".

Easy
Answer: Extravagant. "Frugal" means careful with money or resources; "extravagant" (spending lavishly) is its opposite.

Quick Tips

Attempt easy questions first to bank time, memorise common percentage-to-fraction conversions, and always double-check units (days vs. hours, ₹ vs. %) before submitting an answer. Practice a timed mock every week rather than solving topics in isolation.
